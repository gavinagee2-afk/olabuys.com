SMS Messaging Consent Policy

This page describes how ola collects consent to send text messages (SMS) to its customers.

How customers opt in

Customers of ola provide their mobile phone number at the time of service (in person, by phone, or at booking) and agree to receive a follow-up text message asking them to leave a review of the work performed. Providing a phone number and agreeing to be contacted is how a customer opts in.

What messages we send

After a completed job, ola may send a short text inviting the customer to leave a Google review. Message frequency is low, typically 1 to 3 messages per customer.

Opt out and help

Customers can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, and can reply HELP for assistance. After a STOP request, no further messages are sent.

Rates and privacy

Message and data rates may apply. ola does not sell or share customer phone numbers with third parties for marketing. Numbers are used only to contact the customer about their own service.

For questions about this policy, contact ola directly.

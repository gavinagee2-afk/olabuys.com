Selling a Rental Property

Owning rentals can be rewarding, and it can also be a lot of work. When an owner is ready to step back, a direct sale is one path to consider.

Rental owners we talk with

We hear from many kinds of property owners, including:

  • Owners tired of day to day property management
  • Owners of vacant rentals
  • Rentals that need repairs or updates
  • Owners dealing with difficult rental situations

How the conversation usually goes

Tell us about the property, its occupancy, and its condition. We will review the details and let you know whether it is a fit and what next steps could look like.

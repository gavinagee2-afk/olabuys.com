About Us

A Local Company Working Directly With Homeowners

ola buys houses directly from homeowners in Middletown and surrounding Southwest Ohio communities.

Why we buy properties

Our team works hands-on with properties, from cleanup and repairs to full renovations. Buying directly lets us offer homeowners another option when a traditional listing is not the right fit, and it lets us put work back into houses across our local communities.

We are based in Middletown and work regularly in Franklin, Hamilton, Monroe, Liberty Township, West Chester, Fairfield, and Lebanon.

How we approach sellers

  • Respect

    Every homeowner's situation deserves patience and discretion, whatever the property condition.

  • Clarity

    We explain the process plainly and answer questions directly.

  • No pressure

    You decide whether an offer makes sense. Saying no is always fine.

  • Local care

    We work on properties in the same communities we live and work in.

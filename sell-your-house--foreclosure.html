Exploring Your Options During a Foreclosure Situation

Financial pressure on a home is stressful. Knowing your options, and your deadlines, can help you make a clear decision.

Understanding your situation first

If you have received notices from a lender, it is important to understand the timeline and requirements involved. A housing counselor, attorney, or financial professional can help you understand what applies to you.

Where a direct sale may fit

For some homeowners, selling the property is one option to consider. We can review the property and let you know whether we are able to make an offer, so you have more information as you weigh your choices.

OLA does not provide legal or financial advice. We cannot guarantee that a sale will stop a foreclosure or resolve a financial situation. Please seek professional advice regarding your specific circumstances and deadlines.

The Process

How Selling Your House to OLA Works

A straightforward process, explained plainly. You stay in control of every decision.

Five steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Contact OLA

    Submit your property information online or call us. Share as much or as little as you are comfortable with to start.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Property Review

    We review the property details, location, and condition, and may ask follow-up questions to understand your goals.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Property Visit If Needed

    When necessary, we schedule a walkthrough so we can see the property in person.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Offer

    If the property is a fit, we present a purchase offer. Take the time you need to review it and ask questions.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Closing

    If both parties agree, the transaction proceeds through the appropriate closing process, including title work and required documentation.

Traditional Sale vs. Direct Sale

A balanced look. Listing traditionally may be the better fit for some properties, and a direct sale for others.

CategoryTraditional ListingDirect Sale
PreparationTraditional listings may involve preparation, photography, cleaning, staging, or repairs.A direct sale may involve fewer preparation steps depending on the buyer and property.
ShowingsTraditional sales commonly involve multiple buyer showings.Direct buyers may be able to evaluate the property directly.
FinancingTraditional buyers commonly rely on mortgage financing.A true cash purchase may avoid lender financing requirements.
TimingDepends on the market, buyer demand, financing, inspections, and closing requirements.Direct sales may provide different scheduling options.

Process questions

Do I have to accept an offer?

+

No. You review any offer on your own terms and are never obligated to accept it.

Will someone need to visit the property?

+

Sometimes. When we need to see the property to make an accurate offer, we will schedule a walkthrough at a time that works for you.

How long does closing take?

+

Timing depends on the property, title work, and closing requirements. We will discuss realistic scheduling with you.
